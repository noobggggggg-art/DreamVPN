# DreamVPN

**DreamVPN** is a user-friendly and sleek Windows VPN client featuring an interactive 3D globe interface. The app works with your own keys and subscriptions (Bring Your Own Server).

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Interactive Globe:** Your servers are beautifully displayed on a globe, grouped by country.
* **Secure TUN Mode:** All traffic routes safely through a system TUN adapter without cluttering local proxy ports.
* **Wide Protocol Support:** Works with popular protocols (`VLESS`, `Trojan`, `Hysteria2`, `Shadowsocks`, `VMess`) and subscription links.
* **Organized Folders:** Keep your subscriptions neat with collapsible folders.

---

## 📥 Download & Install

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/noobggggggg-art/DreamVPN/releases)** page on the right side of the repository.
2. Download the latest installer file (usually a `.exe` file).
3. Run it and follow the on-screen instructions.

*(The app requires administrator privileges upon launch to properly configure secure network routing).*

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

All your keys, configurations, and subscriptions are stored **strictly on your computer** inside `%APPDATA%\dreamvpn\`. They are never sent anywhere else.

---

DreamVPN is strictly a client-side tool. You are solely responsible for the servers you use and for complying with the laws of your country.

# DreamVPN

---

## 🌟 Возможности

* **Интерактивный глобус:** Ваши серверы красиво отображаются на глобусе с группировкой по странам.
* **Надежный TUN-режим:** Весь трафик идет через защищенное соединение без лишних открытых портов.
* **Поддержка любых ключей:** Работает с популярными протоколами (`VLESS`, `Trojan`, `Hysteria2`, `Shadowsocks`, `VMess`) и ссылками-подписками.
* **Удобные папки:** Сворачивайте и организуйте свои подписки так, как вам удобно.
